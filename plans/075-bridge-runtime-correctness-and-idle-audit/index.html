    Bridge runtime correctness and idle audit¶

    Scope and evidence¶

    The user prioritizes system correctness and efficiency over battery-runtime benchmarking. The Pi is disconnected for other work. Audit the saved runtime journals, status/recovery records and source locally; do not claim access to the complete current Pi journal or deploy without a reachable device. Source baseline: clean 20a6eb0, native f574445.

    Saved evidence covers boot, normal connected/offline polling, Wi-Fi recovery, FTP/printing, and the recent extended idle/power-on cycle. Several captures are filtered or overlap, and older captures precede subsequent fixes. Distinguish observed events from source-derived findings. Personal photo data and raw journals remain outside Git.

    Findings and implementation¶

    1. A failing blocking FFI worker can replace its caller's cancellation during the cleanup grace period. A completed worker raising TimeoutError can also be mistaken for a stuck worker. Preserve cancellation after consuming the completed worker's error, retain serialization until actual completion and keep the existing deadline for genuinely stuck workers. The print sender watchdog has the same TimeoutError ambiguity: inspect completed results before reporting a slow or stuck print, preserving a sender's own timeout as a failure.
    2. Obsolete Printer polls can apply failure/recovery side effects despite their generation being superseded. Bond-reset cancellation is swallowed, and cancelled poll tasks are dropped from shutdown ownership. Reject stale results before side effects, propagate cancellation and retain retired task references until cleanup.
    3. Unchanged status is logged at INFO on every poll; ordinary Printer absence produces repeated warnings and scan progress. Keep state changes/recovery and genuine errors visible, move unchanged observations/expected repeated absence to DEBUG, preserving scan frequency, status I/O, freshness and Printer recovery policy. Keep first/value-change/recovery status records and a five-minute healthy heartbeat at INFO so quiescent operation remains observable. First expected absence is INFO; recurring not-found scans are DEBUG. Late-stage connection failures remain visible with their stage.
    4. The render maintenance timer wakes every 0.35 seconds even with the screen off. About collection can also continue while its page is hidden by the lock. Use a slower dark maintenance cadence bounded by freshness deadlines, reschedule on display transitions, and avoid starting hidden About collectors. Awake interaction and networking remain operational.

    Validation and limits¶

    Reproduce the cancellation, stale-result and hidden-collector cases before fixing them. Use controlled worker/events, cancelled recovery tasks and fake clocks to verify ordering, cleanup ownership, genuine error visibility, TTL expiry and prompt wake. Run touched-file formatting, Ruff, strict mypy, the complete Bridge suite and strict docs. Audit affected shutdown/printing/network paths for regressions.

    No native ABI, image bytes, Bluetooth pacing, config/schema, sync API, App or iOS change is planned. Do not infer energy savings or hardware performance from timer/log reductions. Hardware smoke and the complete live journal audit remain unavailable while disconnected.

    Audit coverage and results¶

    Reviewed available boot journals, runtime status/recovery captures, FTP/print traces and the prior hardware audits alongside current startup, printing, FFI serialization, UI polling, shutdown ownership, power scheduling, image-worker/cache, FTP spool and manager-listener code. The saved log inventory summarizes event counts and ranges without committing raw journals. It contains overlapping and filtered sources; aggregate counts are not a complete uptime or error-rate measurement. Several older warnings describe behavior fixed in earlier plans.

    The startup branches already overlap independent CPU helper, FTP, UI and Bluetooth work. Network callers share bounded probes and recovery backoff. Image preparation stays off the event loop with source/cache ownership retained through cancellation. FTP upload ownership and manager listener retry/cleanup remain intact. The main queue's bounded wait also checks FTP listener failure; it was not lengthened at the expense of shutdown/failure detection. Existing bond reset and silent-link recovery policy was retained because it has verified hardware purpose; this audit fixes obsolete/cancelled callers, not Printer protocol policy.

    An offline replay of the 25 saved unchanged healthy status records produced 1 INFO / 24 DEBUG records while still making 25 fetches and preserving readiness. This is a mocked I/O replay, not a hardware or energy benchmark. Dark maintenance's nominal maximum interval changes from 0.35 to 5 seconds; expiry deadlines and user wake still take precedence.

    Eighteen new regression cases cover worker failures during cancellation (including its own TimeoutError), sender timeouts before/after the slow notice, stale failure generations before and during fetch, bond-reset cancellation, retired cleanup ownership, TTL expiry while dark, prompt wake, hidden About reads, unchanged/changed status, sparse heartbeat and genuine error visibility/stage tracking. Focused checks passed; the complete Bridge suite passed 1,542 tests. Ruff, strict mypy (65 source files), touched formatting and strict docs are the final gates. Validation host: macOS / Python3.13.9, mock I/O; no Pi or Printer accessed in this audit.

    The candidate is committed/pushed for review. It is not deployed while the Pi is disconnected. Installed native f574445 and its firmware runtime are unchanged. On a future connection, verify real lock/wake, Print/Sync transitions, cancellation/reconnect and a full runtime journal before claiming hardware efficiency or complete live-log acceptance.

    Subsequent live acceptance¶

    The Pi was reconnected later on 2026-10-12. Plan076 records deployment of these changes, review of all retained application/service journals plus the current kernel journal, and real-runtime lock/unlock, FTP print and recovery checks. The offline counts above remain unchanged historical evidence. Additional native discovery, startup bond and network readiness fixes are recorded in plan076 rather than attributed to this offline audit.
